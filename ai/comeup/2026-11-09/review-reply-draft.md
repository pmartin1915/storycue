Thank you for the review. Here is the information requested.

1. Screen recording: attached. It is recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting at app launch, and shows the typical flow: choose a deck, record an answer, move to the next question, finish, then open Recordings to play, keep or delete a clip. The app has no account, no sign-in, no purchases and no user-generated content shared with other people.
2. Purpose and audience: Tellfolk helps families record a relative answering interview questions on video, for example a grandparent's life story. One person holds the phone and records; the other answers the on-screen question.
3. Setup and sample files: none required. Launch the app, choose a deck, grant camera and microphone access, and tap Record.
4. External services: none. The app makes no network requests and uses no third-party SDKs, analytics or AI services. Recordings stay on the device unless the user exports a clip to Photos or the share sheet.
5. Regional differences: none.
6. Regulated industry: not applicable.
