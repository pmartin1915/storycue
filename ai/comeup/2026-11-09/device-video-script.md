# Tellfolk device video script (about 90 seconds)

Record on the real iPhone with Control Center screen recording. Start recording BEFORE launching, so the video begins at app launch. Use the build shown in ASC (build number is in the lane report). Latest iOS.

1. Home Screen. Tap Tellfolk (video starts here, launch visible).
2. "Choose a deck" appears. Tap "Grandparents".
3. "Before you start" screen: pause one second so the line "Recordings stay on this phone. Nothing is uploaded." is readable. Continue.
4. Allow Camera and Microphone when asked.
5. Read-aloud prompt: say the line out loud. Tap Record. Answer the first question for about 10 seconds.
6. Tap Next question, record a few seconds, then Finish.
7. Recordings list: "Recordings" opens. Tap the new clip; let it play 3 seconds.
8. Keep one clip. Delete another (shows "Recording deleted").
9. Optional: open Export, show the export sheet, cancel. (Nothing is posted anywhere.)
10. Stop the screen recording. Attach it to the Resolution Center reply.

Say in the reply: no account, no purchases, no network use.
